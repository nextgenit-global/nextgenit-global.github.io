TravelX: SEO & Content Transformation

Industry: Travel & Tourism Duration: 6 months Services: SEO, Content Strategy, Technical Audit Team size: 4

Client Background

TravelX is a mid-sized travel blog and booking affiliate platform. They had a strong domain authority but lost 60% of organic traffic after a Google core update. Their content was outdated, and technical errors slowed down the site significantly.

Problem & Challenge

Major drop in search rankings, high bounce rate (78%), slow loading times (LCP > 4s), and no clear content strategy. Client goal: recover traffic and increase affiliate revenue within 6 months.

Research & Planning

We conducted a full technical SEO audit, competitor gap analysis, and keyword mapping. Identified 1200+ broken backlinks and 350+ pages with thin content. Planned a phased approach: technical fixes → content refresh → link building.

Solution Implemented

Rebuilt site architecture, migrated to faster hosting, implemented lazy loading and CDN. Revamped 200 core articles, created 80 new pillar pages, and launched a blogger outreach campaign. Also added schema markup for rich snippets.

Process / Workflow

Discovery (2 weeks) Planning (1 week) Design & Tech Fix (3 weeks) Content Production (8 weeks) Link Building (ongoing) Testing & Launch (1 week)
